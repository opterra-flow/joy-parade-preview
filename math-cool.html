How the mood carries into the ask

The mood of the room at the ask, with and without a Joy Parade before your biggest pitch. This line illustrates the idea. It is not data.

What happens in the room

  1. The room shifts

    We invite everyone to move together, and we go first. People stop watching and start taking part.

    Why it matters to you

    Your next speaker walks on to a room that has already shifted.

  2. The body feels good

    When people move together, the body releases its own feel-good chemistry.

    The research

    Dancers who moved in sync showed a stronger endorphin response, the body’s own reward chemistry. It was measured indirectly, through pain tolerance.Tarr, Launay & Dunbar (2015, 2016)

    Why it matters to you

    A room that feels good wants to stay, and is open to what comes next.

  3. Strangers become a group

    People feel close to the people around them, in minutes instead of days.

    The research

    New singing groups felt close to each other sooner than craft and creative-writing groups. The others caught up in time, so the difference was speed.Pearce, Launay & Dunbar (2015)

    Why it matters to you

    You do not have months to build rapport. A shared activity gets a group there sooner.

  4. Trust follows

    People who have moved together start to work together and rely on each other.

    The research

    People who moved in sync cooperated more with each other afterward. The feeling of being in sync also raised trust.Wiltermuth & Heath (2009). Launay, Dean & Bailes (2013)

    Why it matters to you

    Pitches, partnerships and sponsor halls run on trust. A room that trusts each other is ready to listen.

  5. They are ready to say yes

    A good mood changes what people do next, including what they are willing to spend.

    The research

    Mall shoppers who rated themselves one notch happier spent about 12% more. In another study, an emotion from an earlier, unrelated moment changed what people would pay.Babin & Darden (1996), as reported in Polman (2025). Lerner, Small & Loewenstein (2004)

    Why it matters to you

    Put the good mood right before your biggest ask.

  6. Now put your numbers on it

Each step comes from a different study. No single study followed the whole chain, and these are not results we promise in your room.
