what moving together does to a room.

01

Endorphins

Moving, laughing and singing together releases endorphins, the body’s reward chemistry.

For youA room that feels good is a room that wants to stay.
Cohen et al. (2010). Tarr, Launay & Dunbar (2015). Dunbar et al. (2012).
02

Connection

In new classes of strangers, singers felt close within a month. Craft groups took three.

For youAttendees stop being strangers while your event is still on.
Pearce, Launay & Dunbar (2015). Hove & Risen (2009).
03

Confidence

Moving in sync raised self-esteem.

For youAttendees walk into your next session feeling better about themselves.
Lumsden, Miles & Macrae (2014).
04

Trust

People who moved in sync cooperated more with each other afterward.

For youYour workshops and breakouts start with a room that already works together.
Wiltermuth & Heath (2009).
05

Buying

The mood people arrive in changes the decisions they make next, even decisions that have nothing to do with it. In a real-money study, a feeling from an earlier moment changed what people would pay.

For youPut the good mood right before your pitch, your ask or your sponsor hall.
Lerner, Small & Loewenstein (2004). Lerner, Li, Valdesolo & Kassam (2015).

Endorphins are measured indirectly, through pain tolerance.

Keeping a room engaged isn’t easy

After a long day of sitting, with phones and distractions pulling at everyone, attention naturally drifts. A short, intentional energizer break brings people back.

For youYour next speaker walks on to a room that is back with them, including the people who had been on their phones.
Researchers measured how many students were on task in class before and after a short, structured movement break. After the break, on-task behavior rose 8% overall. Among the students who were least on task beforehand, it rose 20%.Mahar et al. (2006), Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise
